# Jarvis Project Evolution Roadmap 🚀
*A phased, real-world engineering blueprint inspired by Tony Stark's J.A.R.V.I.S.*

---

## 🟢 Phase 1: Core Intelligence & Omnipresence (COMPLETED)
- [x] **Cognitive Brain**: Google Gemini with multi-model failover, proactive advisor protocol, and Indian contextual awareness (IST, INR, local news/weather).
- [x] **Mobile Companion**: 24/7 Telegram bot with voice messages, text, settings, and Google Workspace integrations.
- [x] **Hands-Free Desktop Voice**: Local ONNX wake-word detection (`"Jarvis"` / `"Hey Jarvis"`), adaptive acoustic calibration, neural Edge-TTS voice replies, and procedural audio chimes.
- [x] **Always-On Windows Service**: Windowless background launch (`pythonw.exe`), Windows startup link (`Jarvis.lnk`), and single-instance socket protection.
- [x] **Cloud & Local Dual Mode**: Headless cloud resilience (Render/Railway container auto-detection) paired with native Windows desktop voice.
- [x] **Persistent SQLite Memory**: Notes, tasks, user preferences, dynamic settings, and continuous self-evolution.
- [x] **System Security & Integrity Protocol**: Hardcoded safety protocols guaranteeing reputable, tamper-proof, audited software standards.

---

## 🟡 Phase 2: Laptop OS & System Commander (UPCOMING)
*Empowering Jarvis with direct, safe controls over your laptop hardware and everyday operating system tasks.*
- [ ] **Hardware & Telemetry Monitoring**:
  - Voice queries for battery level, charging status, CPU/RAM usage, and available disk storage.
  - Low battery voice alerts and high-temperature warnings.
- [ ] **Native OS Audio & Display Controls**:
  - Hands-free volume adjustment (`"Jarvis, volume 40%"`, `"Jarvis, mute"`).
  - Screen brightness adjustment (`"Jarvis, dim the screen"`).
- [ ] **Application & Window Launcher**:
  - Launching apps on command (`"Jarvis, open VS Code"`, `"Jarvis, open Chrome"`, `"Jarvis, open Spotify"`).
  - Quick website launcher (`"Jarvis, open YouTube"`, `"Jarvis, open GitHub"`).
- [ ] **Workstation Security Controls**:
  - Instant security lock (`"Jarvis, lock the laptop"`).
  - Power management (`"Jarvis, sleep mode"`, `"Jarvis, restart"` with confirmation).
- [ ] **Clipboard Intelligence**:
  - Reading or summarizing whatever is copied on the clipboard (`"Jarvis, explain what is on my clipboard"`).

## 🟠 Phase 3: Visual Perception & Multimodal Intelligence
*Giving Jarvis eyes using your laptop webcam and live screen capture.*
- [ ] **Webcam Sight ("Jarvis, what am I holding?" / "Look at this")**:
  - Captures a frame from your webcam on voice command and analyzes it with Gemini multimodal vision.
  - Can read physical documents, inspect objects, or describe surroundings.
- [ ] **Screen Awareness ("Jarvis, explain what is on my screen")**:
  - Instant desktop screenshot analysis for reviewing code, debugging errors, summarizing charts, or drafting replies.
- [ ] **Presence Detection**:
  - Optional face / presence verification to greet you when you sit down at your desk.

## 🔵 Phase 4: Stark Holographic Desktop HUD & Waveform Visualizer
*A futuristic, minimalist visual interface on your Windows desktop.*
- [ ] **Transparent Desktop HUD**:
  - A sleek, floating, dark-mode transparent HUD widget on your desktop (customizable or minimized).
  - Circular Arc-Reactor visualizer that pulses and glows when Jarvis is listening, thinking, or speaking.
- [ ] **Live Glanceable Dashboard**:
  - Shows current IST time, live weather & AQI, upcoming Google Calendar meeting countdown, and high-priority tasks.
- [ ] **Quick Mute & Privacy Toggle**:
  - Clickable icon to temporarily mute the microphone when you want complete silence/privacy.

## 🟣 Phase 5: Smart Environment & IoT Automation
*Transforming your room into a Stark-style automated environment.*
- [ ] **Smart Home Bridge (Home Assistant / Tuya / Matter)**:
  - Voice control over smart lights, lamps, fans, and AC (`"Jarvis, lights out"`, `"Jarvis, set AC to 24 degrees"`).
- [ ] **Automated Routines**:
  - `"Jarvis, movie mode"`: Dims lights, sets laptop audio, closes work apps.
  - `"Jarvis, focus mode"`: Blocks distracting apps, opens work workspace, sets focused lighting.
  - `"Jarvis, good night"`: Locks laptop, confirms tomorrow's first meeting, turns off lights.

## 🔴 Phase 6: Autonomous Executive Chief of Staff
*Proactive autonomous workflows that anticipate your needs before you ask.*
- [ ] **Meeting Preparation Protocol**:
  - 15 minutes before any Google Calendar meeting, compiles an executive dossier (recent email threads with attendees, shared docs, key talking points).
- [ ] **Two-Phase Email Drafts & Responses**:
  - Auto-drafts suggested email responses for urgent messages, reads them aloud, and sends upon your verbal confirmation.
- [ ] **Automated Daily Research & Briefing**:
  - Researches news, stock/commodity movements, and industry updates according to your personalized profile and reads key highlights during your morning briefing.
